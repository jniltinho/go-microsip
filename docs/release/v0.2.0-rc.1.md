# go-microsip 0.2.0-rc.1 (pre-release, not v0.2.0)

> **This is a release candidate for testing, not a release.** It is the first version of
> go-microsip, the Go rewrite that replaces rust-microsip. Most of what it does was checked in a
> lab and in virtual machines, not on real sound cards or by a person on a real desktop: read
> "What was and was not checked" before you rely on it.

## What changed since rust-microsip 0.1.0-rc.3

- **The application was rewritten in Go**, with a window drawn by the system's web view (Wails v3;
  WebKitGTK 6.0 on Linux, WebView2 on Windows) and a page that loads nothing from the network.
  The Rust code is kept under `archive/rust/` as a reference; it is no longer built or released.
- **The project and the repository are now `go-microsip`** (`jniltinho/go-microsip`; the old
  address redirects). The executable, the settings folder, the database, the keyring entry and
  the environment variables carry the new name, so both applications can be installed side by
  side. Nothing is imported by itself: `go-microsip import rust-microsip` shows what it would
  bring over, and `--apply` does it.
- **New**: seven skins; a ring for incoming calls and a ringback tone, or the audio the PBX plays
  before it answers; a notification for a missed call; a local audio test in the settings; sound
  devices chosen in the settings and used by the next call; contact CSV import with a preview;
  a software bill of materials with every build.
- **Windows** is a portable `.zip` and a bare `.exe`. There is no MSI in this version. Calls over
  UDP and TCP are now checked on Windows, in a VM.
- **Linux** now needs Ubuntu 26.04 or later, or Debian 13, with GTK 4 and WebKitGTK 6.0.

What go-microsip does differently from rust-microsip on purpose, and what it dropped, is recorded
in the [parity decisions](../migration/parity-decisions.md) (start-up time and memory among them:
the window is slower to appear and uses four to five times the memory on Linux).

## What was and was not checked

The owner passed the parity gate for this pre-release on 2026-11-13 with these terms
([record](../migration/parity-signoff.md)): of the 140 behaviors of rust-microsip that go-microsip
was compared with ([matrix](../migration/parity-matrix.md)), 31 are proven, 26 are dropped or
accepted differences, and **83 are partly shown or not shown at all**. Those 83 are the
limitations of this pre-release, and a final version needs them checked.

Checked, by tests that run without a person:

- Registration, calls both ways, DTMF, hold, mute, blind transfer, do-not-disturb, SRTP and the
  failure cases, against a disposable Asterisk (79 scenarios) and FreePBX 17 (10, and 8 more on
  an impaired network).
- The packaged AppImage placing and receiving lab calls through its window, with sound measured
  on virtual sound cards (30 steps).
- The AppImage on clean Ubuntu 26.04 and Debian 13 VMs and the `.exe` and `.zip` on a clean
  Windows 11 VM: start-up, the window, the keyring, notifications, the tray, links, the command
  line and the MCP server, and on Windows calls over UDP and TCP against a lab PBX.

Not checked:

- **Sound on a real sound card or headset**, on any system, and five-minute calls per codec. The
  Windows VM's emulated sound card misses the sound threshold in every run.
- **A person using it**: KDE, a HiDPI desktop, suspend and resume, signing out during a call,
  Focus Assist, the buttons of a notification on a real desktop, a second Windows user, a failing
  Credential Manager. Windows 10 was never tried.
- **TLS and SRTP on Windows**, and TCP, TLS, SRTP and the packaged application against FreePBX.
- **A home router** (no STUN, no keepalive) and any PBX other than Asterisk and FreePBX.
- No call on a production PBX was made with this exact version.

A review, a build and a VM run are not evidence that a call works on your machine.

## Requirements

- **Linux**: x86_64, Ubuntu 26.04 or later or Debian 13, with the system's GTK 4 and WebKitGTK 6.0
  (`libgtk-4-1`, `libwebkitgtk-6.0-4`; part of the default GNOME desktop) and PipeWire or
  PulseAudio. Where FUSE is missing, run the AppImage with `APPIMAGE_EXTRACT_AND_RUN=1`.
- **Windows**: Windows 11, x86_64. Windows 10 needs the WebView2 runtime and is not tested.

## Known limitations

The full list, with what each means, is in the README's
[Limits of this version](../../README.md#limits-of-this-version). The ones most likely to matter:

- One account and one call at a time. No STUN and no SIP keepalive: the PBX has to keep the audio
  passing through itself and answer to the address packets come from.
- **A TLS account registers only with a CA bundle named in its settings** (`ca_file`); the
  system's certificates are not used, where rust-microsip used them. This difference has no
  decision yet and may change.
- An unknown `ui.theme` in the configuration resolves to the system's skin instead of being an
  error, where rust-microsip refused it. Also undecided.
- An account over UDP reads only what comes from the PBX's own address and port.
- No volume control in the window, no ringtone file, no echo cancellation: use a headset.
- The Windows files are **not signed**, so SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC".
  Check the download first (below). Checksums give integrity, not authorship.

```sh

chmod +x go-microsip-0.2.0-rc.1-x86_64.AppImage

Get-FileHash .\go-microsip-0.2.0-rc.1-windows-amd64.zip -Algorithm SHA256
```

## Release gates

A pre-release does not need them; this is where they stand for `v0.2.0`:

- Open: the manual checklist on real machines (task 7.2 of the
  [migration](../../openspec/changes/migrate-to-go-wails/tasks.md)), which covers the 83
  behaviors above, and the decisions on the two undecided differences.
- Open: three tasks of the first milestone (2.1, 2.8, 2.10) whose own five-minute and
  clean-machine measurements were never completed as written.
- The owner's decision for a final version has not been asked for.

## Rollback

Each step of this release can be undone on its own:

- **The release**: `gh release delete v0.2.0-rc.1 --cleanup-tag`.
- **An installation**: go-microsip and rust-microsip do not share files. Remove go-microsip and
  keep using rust-microsip 0.1.0-rc.3, whose settings, database and keyring entry were never
  touched. go-microsip's own database is never downgraded: a newer schema is refused untouched by
  an older build.
- **The repository's name**: rename it back in the repository's settings and set `origin` again
  (`git remote set-url origin git@github.com:jniltinho/rust-microsip.git`).
- **The cutover**: revert the two cutover commits (the move to `archive/rust/` and the Makefile
  after it) and the workflow change; the Rust build then works as before
  ([rehearsal](../receipts/go-m5-cutover-rehearsal-2026-11-13.md)).
