## How the audit was made

Every row of part A of [`docs/migration/parity-matrix.md`](../migration/parity-matrix.md) (the 57 requirements and 83 scenarios of the Rust-era specs at `v0.1.0-rc.3`) was read against the text of its requirement, clause by clause, and against what exists now: the tests, the lab scenarios and their report of 2026-11-12, the window walkthrough of the AppImage, the VM logs, the receipts of M1 to M5. Four read-only passes, one per area, wrote a status and its evidence for each row; every test name and every link they cite was then checked to exist, and the result replaced the matrix's status and evidence columns. Nothing was run again for the audit: a status rests on recorded runs. The rule was strict: a clause that no named test or recorded run shows is not shown.

The audit found one gap that was closed at once: no test presented an agent's grant after its expiry (`TestAnExpiredGrantIsNotActive`).

### The call left behind when a hang-up crosses the answer

The lab's failure above was followed to its cause. When the other side answers a call at the moment the softphone is canceling it, the SIP library reports the call as canceled and returns; the answer gets no ACK and the call no BYE. The softphone's call is over, and the other side, and the PBX between them, stay in a call until someone there hangs up. The softphone now acknowledges such an answer and hangs the call up (RFC 3261, section 15), only when the call was being canceled: an answer the library itself refuses for its codecs it has already ended.

| What | Shown by |
| --- | --- |
| a PBX that answers the call it is asked to cancel gets ACK and then BYE, and the softphone's call ends as canceled, with no sound device opened | `TestAnAnswerThatCrossesTheHangUpIsHungUp` (a PBX of the test's own over UDP; it failed every time before the change) |
| an answer with only a foreign codec is still ended once | `TestAnAnswerWithOnlyAForeignCodecIsNotACall` |
| the lab's crossing scenario in the whole suite | `engine/cancel/crossing-answer` passed in the four full runs made after the change (three on commit `33cfd09`, one on `92a8aad`), 79 scenarios each, none failed ([report](go-m5/asterisk-report.json)); before it, it had failed in two full runs of six |

This was in the softphone before the day's work: the same library call was used before early media, and the suite's earlier runs did not meet the few milliseconds in which it happens. Four full runs without it are not proof that it is gone from the lab; the test with the PBX that provokes it is.

Other things seen once in these runs, none of them followed further: `TestAnAnswerWithOnlyAForeignCodecIsNotACall` once reported no registration within ten seconds (commit `33cfd09`, the whole `make go-test`; it passed in every other run and four times alone); in one walkthrough of the AppImage the virtual sound cards did not answer within a second for the two call subtests; in another, 11 of 993 pieces of sound were missing on the speaker while the page was frozen (over the 0.5 % the test allows), and the "agent control" subtest ended before its softphone had left the PBX, which refused the subtests after it. That last one is the test's: it and "the command line and MCP" now wait, as the others do.
