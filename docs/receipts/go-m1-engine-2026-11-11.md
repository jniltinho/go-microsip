## What the engine scenarios prove

Each scenario drives `internal/core/engine` only through Commands and Events, as a front end will, with `internal/core/ports/memory` standing in for the microphone (a tone) and the speaker (a tone meter).

- Outgoing call to the echo extension over UDP, TCP, TLS with plain RTP and TLS with SDES-SRTP, each with PCMA and PCMU: the tone comes back; the snapshot's `Secure` matches the account.
- Call between two engines (plain and SRTP): incoming call announced with the caller's number, a different tone measured in each direction, remote hang-up ends both sides as normal.
- Hold and resume: neither side hears the other while held and the holder sends nothing; audio returns in both directions. Mute: the other side receives silence frames, the muted side still hears.
- DTMF (RFC 4733) through `SendDTMF`: the PBX reads `123`; a wrong-digits control gets the other tone.
- Blind transfer: the transferring side ends as `transferred` with `TransferSucceeded`; the other party hears the new destination.
- Refusals: Reject (486), do-not-disturb, a second call while busy, cancel while ringing, a plain offer to a secure-media account (488, the sound devices are never opened), wrong password (`unauthorized`, no retry), unknown CA (`certificate`), Dial while not registered.
- Shutdown while established, while dialing and while ringing: exactly one final snapshot, then `Stopped` as the last event, the stream closes, no sound stream stays open; the other side sees the call end.

Unit tests add: the outbox keeps final snapshots and `Stopped` for a reader that is away and merges the rest; the command queue is bounded and `Send` refuses beyond it; an unreachable registrar is retried with the bounded delay; a refused renewal is reported and the account registers again once the registrar accepts it (`TestRegistrationRecovers`, a loopback registrar).

## Defects found by these runs and fixed

1. **Reception stayed silent after a hold** (first full run: `engine/hold-mute/*` failed, 25 of 28). The vendored diago jitter buffer keeps counting losses during a silence, so a stream that resumes is late forever. Patched in `third_party/diago/media/rtp_jitter_buffer.go` (marked `go-microsip:`, listed in its README) and pinned by `TestJitterBufferResumesAfterSilence`.
2. **A hang-up sometimes sent no BYE** (intermittent, `engine/pair/1201-1202`: 2 failures in 4 runs). `180 Ringing` was sent beside `Answer`; when it was written after the `200`, the library kept the 180 as the dialog's response and refused the BYE. The actor now sends the final response only after the 180 is out. A hang-up that is not confirmed is logged.
3. **Data race between a renewal and unregistering** (race detector, `TestRegistrationRecovers`). The endpoint now stops the renewal loop before it registers or unregisters. A re-registration on a live endpoint also restarts the renewal, which it did not.

## Configuration and storage (tasks 3.5, 3.6, 3.7)

Added the same day, same commands (`make go-check go-test`, all packages pass with the race detector):

- `internal/core/config`: 18 tests, the 13 of `crates/config` ported. `make go-headless` also checks that the binary refuses a password-like option with status 2 before parsing it.
- `internal/core/store`: 23 tests, 21 of the 25 of `crates/storage` ported (the control ledger and grants API is not ported yet). They include a database written by `rust-microsip 0.1.0-rc.3` itself (`internal/core/store/testdata/`), a version-1 database upgraded with a private backup, an interrupted upgrade that leaves version 1 intact, a database locked by another connection (`Busy`), a full disk (`Full`) and a call journal recovered after a kill.
- The engine and store suites run in the build container, which has no `DISPLAY`, `WAYLAND_DISPLAY`, `DBUS_SESSION_BUS_ADDRESS` or `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, no `/dev/snd` and no `/run/user` (checked with the same `scripts/go-docker.sh` that runs the tests).
